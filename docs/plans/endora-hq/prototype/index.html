    Office art: Pixel Agents by Pablo De Lucca and contributors (MIT), characters based on MetroCity by JIK-A-4 (CC0); details in assets/CREDITS.txt. A prototype of the board view for Endora's agent company, staged as a startup: you are the board. The engine here is simulated in the page and follows the planned rules: one owner per task, claiming is atomic, wakes coalesce or wait, a tick every few seconds recovers stuck work, budgets are reserved before a run and enforced during it, finished work goes to a different worker for review, and anything that asks first lands on your desk. None of it is live data.
