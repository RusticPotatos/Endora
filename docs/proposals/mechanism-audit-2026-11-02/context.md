## Top 3 by likely payoff from removing or simplifying

1. **Stop offering the 16 unused bridged tools (weather, traffic, sports, news, safety alerts, `city_meetings`, knowledge, Home Assistant, 8 Brave search tools) to team turns.** None was called or receipted in 14 days and 3,482 runs. They are held back in 74% of Claude turns, named in house instructions, and up front in Claude Code turns. They make tool search noisier, which matters because 33% of held-back calls had guessed arguments. Do it with a filter in the team listing; nothing needs deleting.
2. **Remove the briefing's world budget machinery** (weights, compose/drop, "not included" line, `Recalled`/`Resumed`). It has never dropped a piece in 3,372 briefings: the world part is ~685 tokens against budgets of 1,800-5,200. The real briefing size (thread, item, diff, p90 17-23 KB) sits outside it. Remove the dead names too: `tell_story`/lessons branch, `escalate`/`answer_quiz`/`note` in CLAUDE_LOADS, and the `read_notebook` pointer. Also stop loading `chess` in every Claude work turn (HQ already marks it in downtime turns).
3. **Re-test the 6 KB result cut on Claude turns.** D20 caused it: a budget that summed every call's prompt. Since 1 Nov, Claude turns count the conversation once, and out-of-room fell from 119 Claude turns to 0. The cut now fires in 52% of Claude turns (48 of 92 on 2 Nov), on top of the shell's and the node's own 16 KB caps. Removing it for Claude is likely a quality gain at little cost. It needs an A/B first.

Next: on house turns, the gist, the stubs and `find_tools` (~670 lines) only serve house brains, which took 0 runs on 2 Nov. If the money stays with Haiku (memory: "Money to Haiku"), they could all go together. That is the owner's call on whether house turns continue.

## Dangerous to touch

- **Node contract listing and the bridge policy** (`may_see`, `offered_to`, PROFILES, the Confidential/Restricted tier, `only`/`never`, `NEVER_BRIDGED`). This is the safety boundary for what a turn can see and do. Trim the tool list by filtering what is offered to the team, never by loosening `may_see`.
- **Cache breakpoints and the "Claude reads results whole" rule.** Gists and stubs are kept off Claude turns on purpose. Anything that rewrites earlier messages breaks the cached prefix (Haiku reads ~90k cached tokens a run).
- **D30/D27/D19 tool marks** (no `my_turn`/`claim` for a holder, node tools up front, `review` loaded). Each one fixed a measured failure: `my_turn` as the first call fell from 80-127 a day to 6.
- **`keep_work` on out-of-room.** It commits and keeps the sandbox, which is how a continued turn finds its work (D20/D27). Removing the budget without it would lose work.
- **Transcripts/FTS store.** `ask_record`, owner replay and every measurement here depend on it.
