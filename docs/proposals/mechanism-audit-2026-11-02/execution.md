## Top 3 by likely payoff from removing or simplifying

1. **Continuation at the prompt budget, and with it HQ's handover note** (~850 lines). It took $10.39 of $30.76 since 10-27, a third of all spend, over 473 turns, and only 17 of them moved forward. Now that Claude turns get 30 rounds, it has not fired since 11-01. Decide with the `709514fb` end-reason logs on the next blind set, then remove it if turns no longer end at the budget.
2. **Team-loop machinery beyond the solo loop**: the rank climb, hold-for-away (0 uses), the reviewer turn one rank up (~650 lines; $3.56 since 10-27; 62 passed-over reviews), and the items-at-once knob (never set). Round 4 and the full analysis found that this structure "cost money and hours and did not buy quality", and every run since 11-01 is solo. A blind solo-vs-team comparison is the only test still worth running before deleting it.
3. **Wake generators that mostly get refused**: the stall nudge plus stranded recovery (716 recovery wakes since 10-27, 523 refused, 25% of the turns that ran moved forward, many on archived projects; assignment recovery moved 0 of 14). Merge them into one pass that skips archived and in-review work. Also remove the dead code: timers (0 rows, no caller), the consult records (0 since 10-27), the legacy wake reasons and `COLLEAGUE_HELP`, and the 6 disabled lead routines.
