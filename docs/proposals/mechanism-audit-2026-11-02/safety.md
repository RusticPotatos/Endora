## Top 3 by likely payoff (remove or simplify)

1. **Per-member shares, borrowing and the "out for the day" allowance card** (~300 lines plus desk traffic). Seventeen cards in 14 days, 14 approved: the owner is asked to approve spending the pool already allows. The day-pool ceiling, the per-turn cap and the relay's per-call cap enforce the real money policy. Simplify to the pool plus the per-turn cap. It is money, so it is the owner's call, and the ledger replay above settles it.
2. **Confidential taint lineage** (~400 lines, the `input_origins` and `run_confidential_inputs` tables, triggers). Since ADR 0141 it gates nothing, and 89% of turns carry the mark (unknown origin fails closed), so it says nothing. Keep the per-receipt `confidential` flag and the Restricted guard, drop the inheritance. The owner said "marks stay" in 0141, so confirm with them.
3. **The money cap and model gate exist twice** (relay `most_a_turn`, `may_use` keyed by retired roles, against HQ `money::most_a_turn_of(brain)`). The role gate cost 37 failed turns from 10-25 to 10-28. Make one brain-keyed table that both use. Smaller cleanups beside it: remove `approval_serves` (0 uses) and the same-ask merge (0 rows ever), and mark 0133's coordination budget and 0140 as superseded in their ADRs. Their code is already gone.

## Dangerous to touch

- **The relay's fences:** `unmetered`, the betas filter, `for_claude_code`, `affordable_output`, one call at a time per turn, and `stays_home`. They are the only things between a model's request and the owner's Anthropic key, money and the internet.
- **The push path's refusals** (`hq/` only, no `+` or `--force`, never main, master or upstream, only the stored head, outside repositories never pushed), **`forbidden_path`** and **the privacy scan**. The fork is public, and the scan has never held a branch, so its catch rate is unproven, not proven safe.
- **Restricted never offered**, and the default that an unlisted tool is Restricted.
- **Sealed credentials and `secrets.key`.** Today they are the only at-rest protection, because the vault holds no Endora key (`keys_in_vault = 0`). Don't treat OpenBao as the protection until the owner flips the switch.
- **The runner's container settings**, the drain, the tripwire and the kill switch.
- **Owner's calls found here:**
  - Confidential lab data (227 `container_logs` reads in 14 days) goes to DeepSeek, which no decision explicitly covers. 0141 was framed around Anthropic.
  - Whether the offsite backup takes `secrets.key` along with the DBs.
  - Two module docs on safety paths still state the pre-0141 rule: `bridge.rs` says Confidential goes only to local models, and `stays_home` gives Confidential as its reason. Future agents may act on that text.
- **The office chess board:** not in this area. Owner's call.
