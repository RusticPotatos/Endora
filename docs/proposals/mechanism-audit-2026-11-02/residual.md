## Top 3 by likely payoff from removing or simplifying

1. **The team's hand-offs beyond one worker** (ranks, climb, review one rank up, hand-up, HQ's handover note: ~2,200 lines and $3.55 of review turns since cut-over, before climbs). It is the biggest item, and the experiment record already shows it bought cost, not quality. It needs one blind run with the model held fixed before cutting, because the solo arm also failed.
2. **Turns spent on play**: downtime chess ($4.43) and the model league ($3.99) are **28% of all HQ spend since cut-over** ($8.42 of $30.04). The owner keeps the chess board, so this is the owner's call. The cheap levers are moving chess moves off Sonnet and Opus, and parking the league unless a decision cites it. One chess engine instead of two would also cut lines.
3. **Dead code with no behaviour at stake**, about 1,600 lines that can go in one piece: the bench (~900, and a live relay credential), the butler leftovers in capabilities (~400, 0136 step 5b2), the CEO, ladder and consult leftovers (~250), plus retraction and gaps remnants, `reports_to`, `recalled_at` and `Situation::Recalled`.

## Dangerous to touch

- **The bridge's role table** (`domains/capabilities/src/bridge.rs:84-98`, `way` l.336): it is the only thing in the bridge that keeps read-only roles from act tools. Collapsing `Role` without a replacement read/act gate would quietly open restart-type tools to every turn.
- **`furloughed_at` means "retired"**: about 12 checks keep retired members out of the pool shares, loops, wakes and hand-ups. Dropping the column (rather than renaming it) re-seats Cy, Dee and Zed.
- **Ring 1 and the kept item sandbox** (runner, `endora_sandbox`, keeper volumes): this is the safety fence, whatever 0110's "superseded" label suggests.
- **Old wake rows**: reasons with no enum variant fall back to `LeadReview` when read (`sqlite/schedule.rs:41`). Deleting `LeadReview` needs another fallback, or reading old history breaks.
- **`become_one_team`** (`loops.rs:110-150`) writes roles and furloughs. Re-running it after a role change would act on the new meanings.
- **The Mac's brain**: the owner has a 14B benchmark queued for it. The bench's removal needs the owner's lab change (token file, env, mount).
- **Words in model prompts** (the tester in `turn.rs`, contracts): changing them changes turn behaviour. Treat it as a behaviour change, with a scenario, per the owner's BDD rule.

Unresolved within the time box: whether the briefing budget ever cut anything before cut-over (the replay test above); whether anyone uses the AirPlay 2 door; the league's value to decisions.
