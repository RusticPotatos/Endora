# Area: verification (how the system knows work was done)

Build 49677595. DB window: 2026-10-19 00:00 UTC to 2026-11-02 (HQ idle since ~11:45 UTC 2 Nov). Read-only.

**How the counts were made.** Refusals are counted from `transcripts` (1,497 stored turns; 1,486 since 10-19 against 3,526 runs, so about 42% coverage: every refusal count below is a lower bound). Each `--- call: X ---` was paired in order with its `--- result[: refused] ---`. For each refusal I looked at the rest of the same turn: **re-worded** = the same tool later succeeded with no new reading in between (the refusal only cost rounds); **new reading** = a reading tool (shell, homelab_status, container_logs, repo_*, check...) ran before the success (the refusal *may* have caught something); **never** = no success in that turn. Cost of a round: $10.35 over 4,352 assistant rounds in those transcripts, about **$0.0024 a round** (Haiku).

## Top 3 by likely payoff

1. **The evidence rule's reference format.** About 350 refusals in the 42% of turns that have transcripts, roughly 85% fixed by re-wording alone, about 10 that led to a new reading, and the rate is rising (68 on 11-02). Accepting `dN` and `world:` references and dropping non-references on `post` removes most of them without weakening the per-criterion check. Related: the worker's own shell checks leave no receipt, so the rule pushes models to cite checkouts instead of the checks they actually ran.
2. **The tester/review ladder.** It has been unused since round 4. On record it caught 4 real errors in 6 tester send-backs, but it cost most of round 2's money (D7) and lost four finished tasks in round 3 (D19, D22-D25). Shrinking it to one optional "try the failure case" reviewer turn and dropping accept, the lead step and the send-back cap removes ~900 lines with little expected loss. The 4 catches are the reason not to remove it outright.
3. **repo_submit's untracked-files refusal (D33).** 15 of 17 submit refusals were `__pycache__`, and one lost a held-out task. The fix is one line (`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1` or ignoring byproducts). Close behind: the check's "node answered 404 for the bundle" (4 of 94 checks), and dropping the unused `duplicate_of`/`successor` on closures.

## Dangerous to touch

- **The repo_submit vet** (upstream commits, owner's-values paths, `hq/` only): every repo is public. The one fixture false positive is a reason to tune the path rule, not to remove it.
- **"Only the gate makes done"** (`may_set_status`, `is_done`): without it, HQ's "reported done" is a model's word, and every round's reported-vs-verified comparison loses its meaning.
- **Receipts and transcripts**: every measured decision since 1 Nov rests on them, including the live reply-cap window.
- **Worker turn logging**: a test pins the model's requests byte for byte, and the reply-cap decision is reading these logs right now.
